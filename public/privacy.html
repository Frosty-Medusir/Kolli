YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This notice describes how Kolli handles personal data in line with Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019, and applicable regulations. It is a product notice, not a substitute for legal advice. Before public launch, Kolli’s operator should confirm the lawful bases, retention periods, processor contracts, cross-border safeguards, and any ODPC registration obligations that apply to the actual service.

1. Who is responsible?

The person or entity operating Kolli determines why and how Kolli processes personal data and is the data controller for this service. The operator’s name, postal address, and privacy contact must be published here before collecting data from real users. Until that information is added, do not use Kolli with real personal data. You can also contact the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at odpc.go.ke.

2. Who can use Kolli?

Kolli is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Guest registration asks you to confirm that you are at least 18. Do not use the service if you are under 18. Kolli is not designed to collect children’s personal data.

3. Data we process and why

  • Account and guest details: display name, guest/account status, and (if you choose Google linking) Google account ID, email address, and profile image. We use these to sign you in, link your account, and show your identity to your match.
  • Watch activity: titles selected, match participants, session dates/status, Kosmi invite link, and party count. We use these to find a match, operate the shared room flow, enforce the three-party guest limit, and support feedback.
  • Messages: chat text and sender details are stored to deliver chat. Messages are currently configured for automatic deletion after 24 hours; this deletion depends on the database TTL monitor.
  • Ratings and reports: ratings, optional comments, and any safety reports you submit are used for community safety, feedback, and moderation.
  • Technical data: standard server logs and request metadata may be processed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Do not put sensitive personal information (including health, biometrics, identity numbers, location, or financial details) in your display name, chat, or rating.

4. Lawful basis and choice

Kolli should process only what is necessary for the service you request, for legal obligations, or for a documented legitimate interest that does not override your rights. Where consent is the appropriate basis, Kolli will request a free, specific, informed, and affirmative choice and let you withdraw it. The 18+ and privacy-notice checkboxes confirm those actions; they are recorded with the notice version and time and are not blanket consent for unrelated uses.

5. Who receives data?

Your display name and profile image, the selected title, and messages are shared with your matched party. A Kosmi room invite link is visible to both matched users; do not share a private invite link unless you are comfortable with that. Kolli may rely on hosting/database providers, Google for optional account linking, TMDB for title search, and Kosmi for watch rooms. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy notices. Kolli does not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

6. Transfers outside Kenya

Service providers may process or store data outside Kenya. Before enabling a provider, Kolli’s operator must identify the destination and provider, establish a lawful transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards under Kenyan law, and provide any required information or obtain any required consent. The exact hosting region and provider arrangements must be supplied by the operator; Kolli does not infer or promise a location.

7. Retention

Guest accounts and watch history are retained until the account expires or you request deletion. Match records are configured to expire eight hours after a session is created. Chat messages are configured to expire after 24 hours. Ratings and safety reports may be retained while needed for community safety, resolving disputes, or legal obligations, then deleted or anonymised. These periods must be reviewed against actual operational needs and applicable law. Backups and provider logs may persist for their documented rotation periods.

8. Your rights

Subject to the Data Protection Act, 2019, you may request information about your data, access a copy, correction, deletion, restriction or objection where applicable, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, and complain to the ODPC. Use the controls in your Kolli session to download a copy of your Kolli data or delete your account. For help or a rights request, contact the service operator using the privacy contact that must be published above.

Account deletion removes Kolli account, match, message, rating, report, and queue records associated with that account. Other people’s independent copies, legal retention requirements, and data already handled by external providers may not be erased by this action.

9. Security and breaches

Kolli uses access controls and reasonable technical and organisational measures, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs, the controller will assess and notify the ODPC and affected people within the periods and in the circumstances required by the Act, including the applicable 72-hour notification requirement where it applies.

10. Changes and complaints

We will update this notice when the service or its data practices change and show the latest revision date here. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the ODPC through the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
